# wa

A terminal WhatsApp client. These are the words we use for its chat features.

## Replies

**Reply**:
A message that answers another message.
_Avoid_: response, quoted message

**Original**:
The message a Reply answers.
_Avoid_: parent, source, referenced message

**Quote**:
What a Reply's bubble shows of its Original: the Original's text (or a label for media) and the Quote author.
_Avoid_: preview, snippet

**Quote copy**:
WhatsApp's copy of the Original, sent with each Reply. It can differ from the Original if the Original was edited later.
_Avoid_: snapshot, quoted message

**Quote author**:
The person who wrote the Original.
_Avoid_: participant (WhatsApp's name for it), quotee
